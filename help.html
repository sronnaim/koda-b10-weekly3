How WargaReport Works

Everything you need to know about reporting civic issues, public countdown timers, and tracking city fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How it works

    How does WargaReport get city issues fixed?

    1. 1. You spot an issue (pothole, water leak, dark street) and snap a photo.
    2. 2. WargaReport verifies the evidence and dispatches it directly to the responsible municipal agency (PAM JAYA, Bina Marga, Dishub, DLH, Satpol PP, Citata).
    3. 3. A public countdown starts. Neighbors can upvote the report to increase priority.
    4. 4. Field crews resolve the physical issue and post verification proof before closing.

Status Meanings

What does each status mean?

Submitted
Your report was submitted and received by our intake router.
Received
Verified by WargaReport and queued for agency dispatch.
In review
Agency coordinators are assessing field safety and equipment.
Assigned
A field crew or technician is dispatched to the street.
Awaiting your info
Inspector needs clarification or an updated photo.
Resolved
Physical fix completed and inspection statement published.
Closed
Case completed and archived.

Is this an acute life-safety emergency?

WargaReport is for non-urgent municipal infrastructure. If there is an active fire, medical crisis, or immediate hazard to life:

Call 112 (National Emergency) Call 113 (Fire Dispatch)
WargaReport

WargaReport is an independent civic reporting platform for municipal infrastructure issues across Indonesia. Residents submit evidence, follow SLA countdowns, and track resolution updates from the responsible agencies.

Not for life-threatening emergencies, call 112 (National Emergency Call Center) or BPBD DKI directly.
